About the data

Net favorability: percent with a favorable opinion minus percent with an unfavorable opinion.

Net buzz: percent who heard positive things about the brand minus percent who heard negative things.

Net purchase consideration: percent very likely (or more) to consider purchasing from the brand minus percent not very likely (or less).

Source: Morning Consult Daily Tracker, U.S. general population, pulled Oct 13, 2026. Capital One reflects the tracker's corporate "Capital One Financial" series, the closest match among several Capital One products.

Weekly view: weeks run Monday to Sunday and are labeled by the Sunday. The partial week at the start of August and the two days after Oct 11 are left out. Citi, Visa and Mastercard are left out of the weekly charts because of small weekly samples or because they are networks.

Caution: the reason for the lower Amex sample in recent weeks has not been established. Weekly readings can move several points on sampling alone, and more so for smaller samples.
